This donor is not Find X10 firmware. OEM libraries and tuning retain their
original ownership; the repository MIT license does not cover OEM assets.

The plugin repairs three log strings, two capability-dispatch instructions
and its private support dependency name. The support ELF identity is renamed
to libspstdc.so so the phone's existing libstdc++.so is preserved. Full input
and output hashes and byte offsets are in the repository's docs/patches.json. The engine and all
eight selected tuning profiles are unchanged donor bytes.

The two exact policy captures and effect configurations came from the user's
phone. Existing declarations and routing are retained; one Spatializer output
and its wired/USB routes are added. The installer copies the phone's own exact
libaudiopolicyextimpl.so and patches only the staged copy. The original phone
library is not bundled. The original native enabled/off guard is unchanged.
